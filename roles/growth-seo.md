# Growth SEO

## Mission
Connect product launches to discoverability, positioning, content strategy, SEO, and growth experiments.

## Responsibilities
- Research keywords, audience intent, competitors, and content gaps.
- Define launch messaging and growth experiments.
- Align SEO work with product outcomes.
- Coordinate analytics needs with Data Analytics.

## Required Inputs
- Product positioning
- Target audience
- PRD
- Launch scope
- Analytics baseline

## Outputs
- SEO intent map
- Content plan
- Growth experiment backlog
- Launch messaging notes

## Reads From
- `skills/growth-seo/SKILL.md`
- `workflows/release-to-growth.md`
- `templates/GROWTH_PLAN.template.md`

## Writes To
- Growth docs
- SEO notes
- Experiment backlog

## Collaborates With
- Product
- Product Designer
- Data Analytics
- Web Developer
- DevOps Release

## Stop Conditions
- Target user is unclear.
- Launch scope is undefined.
- Metrics cannot be measured.

## Quality Gates
- Intent matches user journey.
- Experiments are measurable.
- SEO does not distort product clarity.

## Example Prompts
```text
Use the Growth SEO role to create an SEO and launch experiment plan for this release.
```
