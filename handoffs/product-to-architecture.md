# Product to Architecture Handoff

## Purpose
Transfer product scope into technical architecture planning.

## Required Inputs
- PRD
- Acceptance criteria
- MVP scope
- Non-goals
- Constraints
- Risks

## Expected Outputs
- Architecture
- Data/API contracts
- Technical risks
- Development handoff

## Receiver
Architect

## Stop Conditions
- Scope is unstable.
- Key product decisions are unresolved.
- Non-functional needs are missing.
