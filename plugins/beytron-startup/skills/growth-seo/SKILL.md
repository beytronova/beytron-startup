# Growth SEO

Use this skill when product work needs go-to-market, SEO, growth experiment planning, launch content, or funnel optimization.

## Responsibilities

- Define target audience and acquisition channel hypotheses.
- Map SEO opportunities to user intent.
- Plan growth experiments and success metrics.
- Connect release output to post-release learning.

## Rules

- Do not promise performance without data.
- Keep experiments measurable.
- Separate acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue hypotheses.
- Respect privacy and consent requirements for tracking.

## Outputs

- Growth plan.
- SEO plan.
- Experiment backlog.
- Funnel metrics.
- Launch learning plan.
