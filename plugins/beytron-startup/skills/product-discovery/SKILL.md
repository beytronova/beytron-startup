# Product Discovery

Use this skill when a raw idea needs to become structured product discovery output.

## Responsibilities

- Clarify the idea, user, problem, assumptions, and success metrics.
- Route unapproved ideas through discovery before PRD, design, architecture, Jira, or code.
- Produce or revise IDEA, RESEARCH, PRD, and approval drafts.
- Keep work inside an incubator or planning area until repository creation is approved.

## Required Inputs

- Raw idea or existing idea artifact.
- Current approval status.
- Known business context and constraints.

## Outputs

- Research summary.
- PRD draft or revision notes.
- Open questions and risks.
- Approval recommendation.

## Stop Conditions

- Unknown approval status.
- Missing target user or problem definition.
- User asks for code before approval gates are satisfied.
