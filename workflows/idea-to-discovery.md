# Idea to Discovery

## Purpose
Turn a raw idea into a researched opportunity before PRD or code.

## Trigger
A stakeholder proposes a new product, feature, or improvement.

## Required Roles
- Product
- Growth SEO when market/search demand matters
- Data Analytics when success metrics are unclear

## Steps
1. Capture the raw idea.
2. Clarify target user, problem, constraints, and assumptions.
3. Research alternatives, risks, and market signals.
4. Define whether the idea should continue to PRD.

## Outputs
- Discovery summary
- Open questions
- Continue/stop recommendation

## Stop Conditions
- Problem is vague.
- Target user is unknown.
- Evidence is too weak to scope PRD.
