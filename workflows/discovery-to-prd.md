# Discovery to PRD

## Purpose
Convert discovery into a product requirements document.

## Required Roles
- Product
- Product Designer for user flow support
- Data Analytics for metrics

## Steps
1. Read discovery notes.
2. Define problem, users, goals, non-goals, MVP, acceptance criteria, metrics, and risks.
3. Record open questions.
4. Prepare product-to-design and product-to-architecture handoffs.

## Outputs
- PRD
- Product risks
- Handoff inputs

## Stop Conditions
- Acceptance criteria are not testable.
- MVP scope is too broad.
- Success cannot be measured.
